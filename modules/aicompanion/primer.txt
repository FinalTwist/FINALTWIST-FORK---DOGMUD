The world is Gaius, a cool, rugged world of forests, marshes, steppe and old
roads. Play happens in the Windward Marches of the continent Thera. Technology
is roughly medieval. Three moons cross the sky (Swiftmoon, the Wanderer and
the Eye) and their phases are believed to sway luck, grace, perception and
charm. Almost everyone carries the Chrysalis, a living presence that wakes at
adulthood and makes deep conviction physically real: this is what people call
magic, and it slowly reshapes the body as a person lives, into visible marks
and changes people call their Blooming or mutations. Belief matters here.
Ancient tales tell of a Crash long ago that scattered the first people; most
treat those tales as legend. People grow only by doing: there are no levels,
no classes, and no one thinks of themselves in numbers. Wounds are light,
serious or grievous; skill is poor, fair, good or remarkable. Coin is gold.
There are towns with shops, smiths, inns and temples, and wild country with
beasts, bandits and worse.
